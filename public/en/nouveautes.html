Updates

NUR gets better without you having to think about it. The tablet checks once a day whether a new version exists and offers to install it. A Wi-Fi connection is all it takes: nothing to plug in, nothing to download on a computer.

Latest version: 0.8 — November 2026 To see yours: Settings → Help & tools → About. To look for an update right now: Settings → Help & tools → Check for updates.

Version 0.8

November 2026

The details of what changes are shown on the tablet when it offers you the update. Here, each new feature can be seen on video.

On video

  • Arabic letters: eight steps to discover the letters and their sounds, in the voice of a native speaker. A way to get started, a little each day.
  • Your country's prayer times: Paris Mosque, Algeria, Tunisia, Morocco, Turkey, Emirates, Indonesia, Malaysia, each on its authority's calendar.
  • Text size: one setting for the whole tablet, and bigger buttons.
  • The adhan: all adhans at the same level, with the volume adjustable right where you need it.

Version 0.7.8

10 October 2026

A language fix, found while going through the app with a fine-tooth comb.

Fixes

  • The Quran in Bengali. The app offered Bengali, but opening a surah in that language showed an error and not a single verse — not even the Arabic text. The Quran opens again, with its recitation and its commentary in Bengali. The translation of the verses, however, is shown in English until a Bengali translation has been added.
  • The help uses the words on the screen. In eight of the eleven languages, it referred to some buttons and sections by terms other than those actually displayed: you could search in vain for the setting mentioned. Thirty-three wordings have been revised.
  • The three alert modes of a prayer were all announced as "Adhan mode" by the screen reader, whichever mode was chosen.

Version 0.7.7

10 October 2026

The big update since the version shipped with the tablets. It brings together several months of work: if your tablet was still on 0.5.1, this is everything you receive in one go.

New features

  • Six new hadith collections, bilingual Arabic/French: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Abu Dawud, Sunan Ibn Majah, the 40 Qudsi Hadiths and the 40 Hadiths of ad-Dahlawi. The library now holds nearly 28,700 hadiths, all available offline.
  • Two separate volumes: the adhan volume and the Quran recitation volume are adjusted independently. Turning one up no longer affects the other.
  • Download the whole Quran at once, to listen to it offline. The download resumes by itself if interrupted.
  • Bookmarks carry their date. The list is sorted newest first, and a long press on a line offers to delete it.
  • Resume your reading. The first time you open the Quran, NUR asks just once whether you prefer to return to where you stopped or go back to the surah index. The choice can still be changed in the settings.
  • A new settings screen: five sections down the side — Prayer, Adhan, Reading, Device, Help & tools — each showing its current setting, with the details alongside.
  • The app's help has been entirely rewritten, in all eleven languages. It described screens that no longer existed and promised a GPS the tablet doesn't have.

Improvements

  • The screen no longer flickers at every scroll. Fast refresh is now the default setting.
  • Full-screen mode holds. It no longer switches itself off when you unplug the tablet: it stays on until you touch it, or until the battery reaches 15% — enough to keep the energy needed for the prayers.
  • Auto-rotation is off by default. On a tablet sitting on a stand, it cost a dozen hours of battery life. It can still be switched on in the settings.
  • More comfortable Quran reading: page turns align with the lines, the Arabic line spacing has been reworked, and the verse number no longer takes up a line of its own.
  • The first-start assistant has been completely reworked.

Fixes

  • The adhan, on its critical path. An alarm could carry the previous day's time — up to two minutes off, and Fajr sounded twice. A prayer could also stay silent after the clock was reset. Alarms now reschedule themselves after an update, a clock change or a time-zone change.
  • Leaving the settings during the adhan cut off the sound.
  • The app could crash when stopping the adhan.
  • Until the setup assistant is finished, the tablet no longer sounds for a default city.
  • In Arabic, each prayer's name was shown twice and the back arrows pointed the wrong way. Arabic letters came apart from one another on the home screen and in the Quran.
  • The recommended calculation method appeared twice in the list, and none was suggested when the app was not in French.
  • No more "you have the latest version" message when the network is unreachable, and a postponed update is offered again.

Version 0.5.1

June 2026

The version installed on the tablets when they were shipped. Adhan reliability fix.

Hasn't your tablet received the update yet?

The check happens once a day, and only if the tablet is connected to Wi-Fi. You can trigger it by hand from Settings → Help & tools → Check for updates.

The download is around sixty megabytes: leave the tablet connected until it finishes. If something gets stuck, write to us.
