The features, one by one

🕌 Your country's prayer times

You choose your city, and your country's method is suggested first. Paris Mosque, Algeria, Tunisia, Morocco, Turkey, the Emirates, Indonesia, Malaysia: each method follows its authority's calendar.

🔊 The adhan

Nine muezzin voices, a mode for each prayer, the full-screen alert with its stop button, two separate volume levels.

📖 The Quran, offline

The Madinah Mushaf text, three display modes, eight reciters, full download, dated bookmarks and resume reading.

📚 The hadith library

Nearly 28,700 hadith offline: Riyad as-Salihin, Nawawi's 40, Sahih al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah, an-Nasa'i, and Hisn al-Muslim for supplications.

🔤 The Arabic letters

First steps, just like at Quran school: the letter, the vowel, the syllable, the word. Every card can be heard in a native speaker's voice. A way to get started, a little each day.

🔎 Text size

One setting, once, for the whole tablet: normal, large or extra large. The home screen, prayer times, Quran and hadith all follow.
