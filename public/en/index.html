Why NUR exists

Assalamu alaykum. NUR was born from a simple observation: our phones follow us even into our moments of devotion — with their notifications, their social feeds, their distractions. Opening a Quran app too often ends somewhere else.

So we built the object we wanted for our own homes: a paper-like screen in the living room that shows the prayer time, sounds the adhan, and waits for one thing only — for you to open the Quran. Nothing else. That is NUR: a light, without noise.

— The NUR team · assembled and shipped from France

Frequently asked questions

Who makes the tablet?

NUR is the result of a collaboration with Meebook, a well-established maker of e-ink e-readers. The e-ink hardware is made by Meebook; the NUR experience (prayer, Quran, hadith, Qibla) is developed by us.

Where do the texts (Quran, tafsir, hadith) come from?

We do not write any religious content ourselves. The tablet reproduces recognised, publicly available sources: the Quranic text of the Madinah Mushaf (Tanzil project), the Al-Mukhtasar tafsir (Markaz Tafsir), the scholar-supervised HadeethEnc hadith encyclopaedia, and freely distributed recitations. This content is provided as is; if you notice an error, write to us at contact@nur-store.com — corrections are rolled out through free Wi-Fi updates.

What exactly does the tablet include?

Prayer times with the adhan, the complete Quran (text, translation, tafsir and verse-by-verse audio), a hadith library (Riyad as-Salihin, Nawawi, Hisn al-Muslim…), the Qibla, the Hijri calendar, the verse of the day and Ramadan mode. Everything is already installed.

Are the prayer times calculated for my city?

Yes. The tablet connects to your Wi-Fi and you choose your city and the calculation method. The times match your location precisely, and the adhan sounds at every prayer with the muezzin of your choice.

Is a subscription needed?

No. No subscription, no account. You pay for the tablet once and everything is included, including free updates over Wi-Fi.

How long does the battery last?

Thanks to the e-ink screen (like an e-reader), battery life is measured in days, not hours. The screen uses almost no power while showing a static page.

Delivery: how long, and which carriers?

Shipped from France within 48 h. Two options at checkout: Mondial Relay pickup point (€5) or Colissimo home delivery (€10). If you choose Mondial Relay, you select your pickup point right after payment, on the confirmation page. Every parcel is tracked and insured.

Which languages is the tablet available in?

The interface is available in 11 languages: French, English, Arabic, Urdu, Bengali, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Turkish, Indonesian and Swedish.

How does payment work?

Secure card payment via Stripe. Your card details never pass through our site.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, in 4 interest-free instalments with PayPal, selected at checkout. The total stays exactly the price shown — no interest, no fees, no late penalties. The tablet is shipped as soon as you order, without waiting for the remaining instalments. Cannot be combined with the launch offer.

Why PayPal for paying in instalments?

We wanted the amount you pay to remain exactly the price shown, whatever happens. Most solutions on the market charge penalties as soon as an instalment is late; PayPal has removed them. The terms are published by PayPal, so you can check them yourself.
